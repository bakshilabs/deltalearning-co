    In the full prototype

    Each world has six stages. Each stage has three cards, giving 729 possible paths. There is no single right answer. One card at each stage gives unclear instructions, so the AI has to guess.

    About this demo

    Numbers are simplified for the web. The AI helper uses pre-written replies. No live AI model is involved. After each result, the session leader has a question to help children explain what they would change.

    Why organisations use it

    Safe for children. Simple for your team.

    • Safe by design

      Children choose from cards that give the AI instructions. Every response is written and tested in advance. There is no open chatbot and there are no child accounts.

    • Easy to deliver

      Your own staff run the games in your existing sessions. They do not need to be AI specialists.

    • Built around your organisation

      Your subject, your audience and your learning goals shape the story, the missions and what children make.

    Our experiences are still being developed as prototypes. Before any pilot, we agree with each partner how sessions will run, how data will be handled and how children will be kept safe.
