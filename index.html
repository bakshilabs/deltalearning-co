    In the full prototype

    Each world has six stages with three cards at each, so there are 729 possible paths and no single right answer. One card at each stage is vague, so the AI has to guess.

    About this demo

    Numbers are simplified for the web. The AI helper is scripted, so no live AI model is involved. In a session, a facilitator prompt after each result helps children explain what they would change.

    Why organisations use it

    Safe for children. Simple for your team.

    • Safe by design

      Children choose from designed prompt cards, and every response is written and tested in advance. No open chatbot and no child accounts.

    • Easy to deliver

      Your own staff run it, inside sessions you already deliver. They do not need to be AI specialists.

    • Built around your organisation

      Your subject, your audience and your learning goals shape the story, the missions and what children make.

    Our experiences are prototypes in development. We agree the set-up, data handling and safeguarding with each partner before any pilot.
